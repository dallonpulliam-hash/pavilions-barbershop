GOOD TO KNOW

THE DETAILS,
MADE SIMPLE.

A few quick answers before you book your next visit.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking online is the best way to reserve your preferred barber and time. Walk-in availability may vary, so contact the shop before stopping by without an appointment.

How do I book?

Use any “Book on Booksy” button on this website. Choose an available service, barber, and time, then follow Booksy's steps to confirm.

Can I choose my barber?

Yes. Choose your preferred barber through Booksy, subject to availability. If you're new to the shop, browse the team listings to find the right fit.

What is the cancellation policy?

Review the current cancellation and rescheduling terms shown in Booksy when you make your appointment. Contact the shop if you need help changing a booking.

How much does a haircut cost?

Prices can vary by service and barber. The current Booksy menu displays the available services and prices before you confirm your appointment.

What if I'm running late?

Please contact the shop as soon as possible. Your barber may need to adjust or reschedule your appointment depending on the delay and the day's schedule.
